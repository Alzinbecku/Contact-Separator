Contact Separator

Compare two exported contact files and pull out the ones that you want to use. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Developed by Ali Ceesay

1. Upload the files

Export both as .vcf (vCard) from the Contacts app / iPhone Contacts, and drop them below.

phone1 contacts
(the "Last Import" / kept-on-Mac list)
phone2 contacts
(the full, merged list)

2. Review the matches

Contacts found in both files are flagged as likely yours. Uncheck any that are actually hers before exporting.

0total in her list
0flagged as yours
0unique to her
Yours?NameDetails
Checked rows will be excluded from the export; the exported file keeps everything left unchecked.
